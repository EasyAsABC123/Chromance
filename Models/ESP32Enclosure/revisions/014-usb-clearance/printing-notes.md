# Printing and assembly notes

Model geometry in millimeters; no slicer profile or physical testing applied.

- Revision014 relieves only the RESET slider, BOOT slider and BOOT frame for the measured USB plug. The body, lid, bracket, four PCB clamps and five other cartridge pieces are preserved from013, including every board-fit, switch-center and mounting datum.
- Measured PCB is 49 x 70 x 1 mm. Solder allowance is deliberately 6.6 mm to preserve the PCB top at local Z10 and the case rim at Z32; this allowance is a placement choice, not a measured solder height. The 52 mm populated ESP32 span is separate from the substrate.
- Additional total fit width is 0.5 mm. The shoulders are 50.3 mm apart, giving 0.65 mm centered clearance per side. Both shelves and top clamps overlap the centered measured board by 1.3 mm and retain at least 0.65 mm after full lateral play. The vertical capture slot remains 1.2 mm high.
- Button contact centers are 30/16 mm from the PCB bottom edge and 3.3 mm from the left edge. These are user-supplied XY dimensions at nominal centered board placement; actual height, switch travel, PCB lateral play and physical fit require the button coupon.
- The 8.4 mm cylindrical cut forms an open concave groove for the measured 8 mm cable. Radial allowance is 0.2 mm, with 2 mm side walls and 2 mm material above the tie tunnel.
- The cradle extends along the cable for 8 mm. Its underside and tie-tunnel roof use 45 degree slopes. The tunnel is 3.2 mm wide for a provisional 2.5 x 1 mm strap. Thread with the lid open, seat the cable in the groove, and snug the tie over it with the head above the right shoulder.
- Cable diameter is measured; groove clearance, zip-tie dimensions, head size and physical fit remain unverified. Keep slack between the retention point and terminals. Standard ties may need cutting and replacing for removal.
- Print fit-tests/009-usb-clearance first. Its three replacement pieces fit the preserved interfaces; its complete open jig retains the board and cartridge geometry. Reuse board test007 and cable test006 for unchanged interfaces. Record material/settings and fit observations; coupons do not establish full-part strength or warping.
- Normalized original USB chart A/B/C/D/E/F is 10/15/20/9/23/3.5 mm: width, top above PCB, outboard projection, underside above PCB, center from bottom and USB cable diameter. The user clarified raw B6 as thickness6; original diagram B is derived top15 (D9 + thickness6). Active plug thickness is 6 mm. The measured USB cable is separate from the 8 mm end-cradle cable.
- Provisional USB relief clearance is 0.2 mm. Slider relief covers the stationary expanded plug translated upward through the full 0.8 mm stroke and opens below the slider so it can top-load around an installed plug. The affected BOOT forward rail opens upward without a thin top lip. Minimum remaining material to the contact insert envelope is 1.285 mm. Maximum plug width is conservatively applied across its full projection; strain-relief taper and cable axis height remain unmeasured.
- The relief uses nominal centered board placement. Board movement within the unchanged retention gap, actual plug assembly/insertion sequence and clearance through button travel require the quick USB test; do not force a plug past a cartridge.
- PCB edge clamps retain M3x5 heat-set inserts and M3x6 screws, with 4 mm engagement and 2 mm blind-tip margin. No screws pass through the PCB. The capture slot is PCB thickness plus 0.2 mm play; verify bare-edge overlap and solder clearance.
- Install PCB inserts before board/cartridges. The diameter 6 mm tool probe needs at least 21.8 mm narrow reach with 0.1 mm nominal wall clearance. Actual iron access needs checking; remove the BOOT cartridge for left-front screw service.
- Lid joints retain M3x5 heat sets and M3x8 screws in short corner pads with paired 45 degree ribs. Pilot diameter/depth are 4/6 mm; screw engagement is 5 mm with 0.4 mm blind-tip margin. Install flush from the open rim. Do not reuse M3x10 lid screws.
- Four bracket M3x5 inserts use M3x10 screws; two contact M3x5 inserts use nylon M3x12 adjusters and exposed jam nuts. Four cartridge inserts remain M3x4 with M3x18 screws. No printed captive hex-nut pockets remain. M3x5 specifies thread and length, not insert outside diameter: OD4.6/pilot4.0 remain provisional.
- The lid and extended finger pads face downward when mounted; press upward through 0.8 mm nominal travel. Desk contact remains at Z43.2; the bracket and all mounting positions are unchanged from013.
- Print the body floor-down, lid exterior-down and bracket desk-contact-face down. Start with supports off for the cradle/corner ribs and inspect their ramps and short bridges in the slicer.
