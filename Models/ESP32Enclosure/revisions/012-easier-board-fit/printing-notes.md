# Printing and assembly notes

Model geometry in millimeters; no slicer profile or physical testing applied.

- Revision012 adds 0.5 mm total structural width for easier board fitting and extends lower shelves and matching upper clamps by 0.5 mm farther under/over the actual PCB. The measured49 mm substrate width remains unchanged. Body, lid, bracket, four clamps and two sliders need matching replacement geometry; other cartridge pieces retain their shape. Previous revisions are preserved.
- Measured PCB is 49 x 70 x 1 mm. Solder allowance is deliberately 6.6 mm to preserve the PCB top at local Z10 and the case rim at Z32; this allowance is a placement choice, not a measured solder height. The 52 mm populated ESP32 span is separate from the substrate.
- Additional total fit width is 0.5 mm. The shoulders are 50.3 mm apart, giving 0.65 mm centered clearance per side. Both shelves and top clamps overlap the centered measured board by 1.3 mm and retain at least 0.65 mm after full lateral play. The vertical capture slot remains 1.2 mm high.
- The existing button Y layout and 3 mm left-edge inset remain provisional. Confirmed centers at 30/16 mm from the bottom and 3.3 mm from the left need a dedicated actuator revision; this fit adjustment does not claim button alignment. Tips remain relative to the measured PCB edge while slider arms extend to the outward-moving guide.
- The 8.4 mm cylindrical cut forms an open concave groove for the measured 8 mm cable. Radial allowance is 0.2 mm, with 2 mm side walls and 2 mm material above the tie tunnel.
- The cradle extends along the cable for 8 mm. Its underside and tie-tunnel roof use 45 degree slopes. The tunnel is 3.2 mm wide for a provisional 2.5 x 1 mm strap. Thread with the lid open, seat the cable in the groove, and snug the tie over it with the head above the right shoulder.
- Cable diameter is measured; groove clearance, zip-tie dimensions, head size and physical fit remain unverified. Keep slack between the retention point and terminals. Standard ties may need cutting and replacing for removal.
- Print fit-tests/007-board-easier-fit before the full replacement. It derives shelf/clamp interfaces from this source; verify bare-edge contact on both sides of the PCB. The cable-cradle geometry remains unchanged from011, so fit-tests/006-cable-cradle stays representative for that separate interface. Record dimensions, material/settings and pass/fail observations; coupons do not establish full-part strength or warping.
- PCB edge clamps retain M3x5 heat-set inserts and M3x6 screws, with 4 mm engagement and 2 mm blind-tip margin. No screws pass through the PCB. The capture slot is PCB thickness plus 0.2 mm play; verify bare-edge overlap and solder clearance.
- Install PCB inserts before board/cartridges. The diameter 6 mm tool probe needs at least 21.8 mm narrow reach with 0.1 mm nominal wall clearance. Actual iron access needs checking; remove the BOOT cartridge for left-front screw service.
- Lid joints retain M3x5 heat sets and M3x8 screws in short corner pads with paired 45 degree ribs. Pilot diameter/depth are 4/6 mm; screw engagement is 5 mm with 0.4 mm blind-tip margin. Install flush from the open rim. Do not reuse M3x10 lid screws.
- Four bracket M3x5 inserts use M3x10 screws; two contact M3x5 inserts use nylon M3x12 adjusters and exposed jam nuts. Four cartridge inserts remain M3x4 with M3x18 screws. No printed captive hex-nut pockets remain. M3x5 specifies thread and length, not insert outside diameter: OD4.6/pilot4.0 remain provisional.
- The lid and extended finger pads face downward when mounted; press upward through 0.8 mm nominal travel. Desk contact remains at Z43.2, while desk/bracket screw X positions move outward by 0.25 mm per side relative to011. Check desk screw spacing before replacing the bracket.
- Print the body floor-down, lid exterior-down and bracket desk-contact-face down. Start with supports off for the cradle/corner ribs and inspect their ramps and short bridges in the slicer. Sliders require local removable support beneath stepped arms; protect guide and magnet seats. No supports are modeled.
- Use the intended final material/nozzle/layer settings for coupons. Unfilled PETG remains the initial candidate; material choice and settings are not validated here. Geometry 3MFs are not slicer projects or G-code. No print-time, strain-relief-force, mounting-strength or creep rating is assigned.
- Support electronics when opening the downward-facing lid. Return force, contact height/travel, connector envelopes, thermal behavior and RF performance require physical checks; use nylon contact tips. The low-voltage controller is enclosed separately from the external PSU.
- Retain all five CAD inputs and the build/render helpers together. Reference cable/tie/hardware solids are excluded from printable exports; hardware STEP contains simplified proxies and is not a complete fastener assembly.
